跨語言科學資訊檢索：少資源語言的科學文本

Cross-lingual Information Retrieval for Scientific Datasets in Less-resourced Languages

CLingS 是歐洲 CHIST-ERA 2025 年度「Science in Your Own Language」（SOL）主題下的跨國合作研究計畫，由法國國立東方語言文化學院（INALCO）擔任總計畫協調單位，結合法國、臺灣、斯洛伐克四個研究單位，為七種少資源語言建置科學語料庫、訓練語言模型，並開發跨語言科學資訊檢索平台。

CLingS is an international research project under the CHIST-ERA 2025 call “Science in Your Own Language” (SOL). Coordinated by INALCO (France), it brings together four partners in France, Taiwan and Slovakia to build scientific corpora, train language models and develop a cross-lingual scientific retrieval platform for seven less-resourced languages.

    為什麼需要 CLingSWhy CLingS

    英語主導了當代科學的生產與傳播。多語言大型語言模型雖然進步快速，訓練資料與應用設計仍高度偏向優勢語言；少資源語言普遍缺乏科學語料、專屬的語言模型與完整的科學術語，使用這些語言的研究者與學習者難以用母語取得與產出科學知識。CLingS 不以「翻譯成優勢語言」為唯一途徑，而是要讓系統能直接以多種語言檢索、理解與生成科學內容，並保留各語言的語言與文化特性。

    Science is produced and shared mainly in English. Multilingual large language models have advanced quickly, yet their data and design remain skewed toward dominant languages; less-resourced languages lack scientific corpora, dedicated models and complete terminologies. Rather than treating translation into a dominant language as the only path, CLingS aims at systems that retrieve, interpret and generate scientific content directly in multiple languages, keeping their linguistic and cultural specificity.

    七種目標語言——台語、烏克蘭語、白俄羅斯語、愛沙尼亞語、斯洛伐克語、旁遮普語、意第緒語——分屬不同語系，卻有共同處境：都曾經或仍然處於政治弱勢，長期在強勢語言的陰影下發展。CLingS 是 SOL 主題中唯一涵蓋台語的計畫。

    The seven target languages — Taiwanese, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Estonian, Slovak, Punjabi and Yiddish — belong to different families but share one situation: each has developed, or still exists, in a politically weaker position beside a dominant language. CLingS is the only project in the SOL call that covers Taiwanese.

    研究主軸What We Do

    01

    科學語料庫Scientific corpora

    為七種語言建置並標註科學語料庫，涵蓋語言學、醫學、數學、地理、法律等領域，每種語言 100–200 萬詞。

    Annotated scientific corpora for seven languages — linguistics, medicine, mathematics, geography, law — 1–2 million tokens each.

    02

    語言模型Language models

    為各語言訓練科學領域的語言模型，並對齊到共享的多語言向量空間。

    Science-adapted models for each language, aligned in a shared multilingual embedding space.

    03

    跨語言檢索Cross-lingual retrieval

    以 Graph-RAG 與多智能體架構打造檢索平台，整合於 INALCO 的 LaCAS 平台。

    A retrieval platform built on Graph-RAG and multi-agent architectures, hosted on INALCO’s LaCAS.

    04

    術語與開放資源Terms & open resources

    建立雙語科學術語對照，邀請語言專家與使用者社群驗證，所有資料集、模型與工具以開放授權釋出。

    Bilingual scientific glossaries validated with experts and communities; all datasets, models and tools released under open licences.

    Taiwan Subproject

    臺灣子計畫：台語科學論述Taiwan Subproject: Science in Taiwanese

    台語曾是臺灣的共通語，但在 20 世紀後半葉長期受到語言壓制，正式場域、教育與學術領域的使用大幅萎縮。2019 年《國家語言發展法》確立台語的國家語言地位，但台語在科學等高階語域仍嚴重不足：科學文本稀少、術語系統不完整、數位資源落後。臺灣團隊由國家教育研究院語文教育及編譯研究中心執行，負責建置台語科學語料庫、台華科學術語對照與台語科學語言模型，並將台語檢索整合至跨語言平台，發展科學教育應用。

    Taiwanese was once Taiwan’s lingua franca, but decades of language suppression in the late 20th century sharply reduced its use in formal, educational and academic settings. The 2019 Development of National Languages Act recognised Taiwanese as a national language, yet it still lacks scientific texts, a complete terminology and digital resources. The Taiwan team, based at NAER’s Research Center for Translation, Compilation and Language Education, builds a Taiwanese scientific corpus, Taiwanese–Mandarin terminology and a Taiwanese scientific language model, integrates Taiwanese into the cross-lingual platform, and develops applications for science education.

    International Workshop 2027 · Taipei

    2027 CLingS 跨國工作坊2027 CLingS International Workshop

    CLingS 法國、臺灣、斯洛伐克三國團隊齊聚臺北，分享少資源語言科學語料庫、語言模型與跨語言檢索的最新進展，並與臺灣的語言學、資訊科學及教育界交流台語科學論述與 AI 應用。

    The French, Taiwanese and Slovak teams of CLingS meet in Taipei to share progress on scientific corpora, language models and cross-lingual retrieval for less-resourced languages, and to discuss science in Taiwanese and AI applications with Taiwan’s linguistics, computing and education communities.
